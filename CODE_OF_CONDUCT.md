# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and free from harassment. Everyone who contributes, opens an issue, reviews code, or participates in discussion is expected to support that environment.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming, respectful, and professional language.
- Focus feedback on ideas, implementation, and outcomes rather than people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions when context is missing.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and levels of technical familiarity.
- Keep discussions relevant to improving the project and its documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, including spam or bad-faith issue reports.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise address contributions and comments that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

To report a concern, open a private vulnerability report if the concern is security-related. For non-security conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the repository owner profile. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and as confidentially as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
